# Design Context

## Audience
CareerSense serves job seekers who need a resume workflow that feels credible, fast, and lower-stress. Primary users include students, early-career professionals, career switchers, and experienced candidates tailoring applications for specific roles.

## Context
Users arrive with high stakes: they want to import, improve, tailor, and export a resume without losing formatting or confidence. The product should feel like a focused career workspace, not a generic SaaS landing page.

## Emotional Goal
The interface should make users feel guided, capable, and in control. It should reduce anxiety around resume quality while signaling professional polish and trust.

## Brand Feel
CareerSense should feel precise, premium, institutional, and career-focused. The visual language should borrow from high-end capital markets and trading education: deep navy foundations, institutional blue panels, champagne-gold accents, crisp business-school typography, chart/grid details, and confident SaaS usability.

## Design Principles
- Use one coherent finance-grade surface system across landing sections and builder screens.
- Prioritize clarity and trust over decorative noise.
- Keep CTAs direct and workflow-oriented.
- Make diagrams and previews feel like product artifacts, not screenshots pasted into the page.
- Make deep navy and institutional blue visually present across the product; use gold as an accent, never the dominant color.
- Avoid teal, emerald, purple, neon blue, crypto/gaming styling, and generic SaaS gradients.
- Preserve strong contrast and readable type in light and dark themes.
